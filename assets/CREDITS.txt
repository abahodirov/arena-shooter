ARENA SHOOTER - asset credits
=============================

All game art and audio in this folder are third-party free assets. Nothing here
was drawn or recorded for this game.

3D models, sprites, UI art and sounds - Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0) - http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

  fps/         Starter Kit FPS            https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-FPS
               blasters, flying enemy, platforms, walls, grass, clouds
  sprites/     Starter Kit FPS            crosshairs, hit marker, muzzle burst, skybox, shadow
               Starter Kit 3D Platformer  particle
  arena/       Mini Arena                 https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-Basic-Scene
               soldier character, walls, columns, statue, blocks, trees, sword
               (soldier-fixed.glb is character-soldier.glb with its two skins merged
                and unused animations removed so the Panda3D glTF loader can read it)
  city/        Starter Kit City Builder   https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-City-Builder
               buildings, roads, pavement, trees, fountain, city ambience sound
  platformer/  Starter Kit 3D Platformer  https://github.com/KenneyNL/Starter-Kit-3D-Platformer
               coin, coin/fall sounds
  ui/          UI Pack                    https://github.com/ereborstudios/kenney-ui-pack
               buttons, checkbox
  sounds/      Starter Kit FPS, Starter Kit 3D Platformer, Starter Kit City Builder,
               Interface Sounds (ui_*.wav) https://github.com/Calinou/kenney-interface-sounds
